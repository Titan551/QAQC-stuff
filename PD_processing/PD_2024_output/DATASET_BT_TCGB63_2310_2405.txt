Sampling and Analytical Methodology:  
Sensor- HOBO U22-001 Water Temp (Onset Computer Corporation, Bourne, MA, USA).  Serial number- 21828445, 20733090
Lower mesophotic coral reef on well-developed agaricid fringe, Sensor at beginning of transect #2, exposed. 
Data Processing:  
Data were offloaded with portable base station to laptop. Data were exported to Microsoft Excel and  visually inspected for unusual deviations. Data taken outside of deployment, during deployment or  retrieval, were snipped from the record by visual inspection of the data. Time stamps were modified  as zone UTC-4 and adjusted as necessary. Records from probe 1 and probe 2 were combined into a  single CSV file.
