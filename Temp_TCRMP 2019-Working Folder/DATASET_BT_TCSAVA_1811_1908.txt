Sampling and Analytical Methodology:  
Sensor- HOBO U22-001 Water Temp (Onset Computer Corporation, Bourne, MA, USA).  
Serial numbers-  10587888
Fringing coral reef near uninhabited Savana Island. Sensor on exposed steel stake of old sediment trap emplacement below transect #1.


Data Processing: 
Data were offloaded with portable base station to laptop.  Data were exported to Microsoft Excel and visually inspected for unusual deviations.  Data taken outside of deployment, during deployment or retrieval, were snipped from the record by visual inspection of the data.  Time stamps were modified as zone UTC-4 and adjusted as necessary.
